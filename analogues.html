What nearby markets can teach

Four neighbours. Only one is the product.

Leo’s story borrows status from prediction markets, from a paper on search and stocks, from Bloomberg, and from meme coins. Each borrow is partly fair. The useful part is smaller than the sentence it arrived in.

AnalogueWhat transfersWhat does not
Prediction exchanges People will bet on an event, at a large face value, and big firms will pay to pass those odds around. ICE putting money into Polymarket is the clear example. Susquehanna making markets on Kalshi shows real trading desks will show up. The size. It is mostly sports, then politics and crypto. Culture sits in “other,” and “other” is what is left over. A yes-or-no bet that ends on a date is not a score that keeps moving.
Prediction screens The job “one clean feed, a saved list of trades, and a way for software to pull it” has a posted price. Oddpool showed a two-person Y Combinator company can ship that and get a meeting with the exchange. The meeting ended with them joining Kalshi. A lasting independent subscription. New Oddpool plans are closed. Y Combinator lists the company as inactive. The exchange took the product inside. That can happen to Tendex too.
Search volume and stocks Google search is a direct measure of ordinary investors paying attention. It is not the same as news or trading volume. In Russell 3000 stocks, from 2004 to 2008, a rise in search came before higher prices for about two weeks, then a fall back within the year. The effect was strongest in small stocks that ordinary people trade. The Federal Reserve did not publish this. The paper is Da, Engelberg and Gao, Journal of Finance, October 2011. The data is old. It stays because it is the paper people mean. It does not show that a culture score insures a brand, or that the effect still works once everyone can see it.
Research screens Koyfin’s own page shows a serious hobby researcher paying $39 or $79 a month on a yearly plan. Unusual Whales starts at $50 for a faster options feed. People already pay for a view their broker does not give them. A $24,000 Bloomberg seat. Public pages do not agree that price, and Tendex does not have the coverage, the compliance, or the existing customers that rent pays for.
Meme coins A price can leave profits behind and follow what people are talking about. Ben already knows how to get a crypto product in front of people. The product itself. A coin’s price is the attention. An index bet is supposed to pay out on a score that lives outside the coin. Mixing the two in a Forum conversation makes Tendex sound like a casino skin. Forum’s public stance is the opposite: a path toward a regulated US exchange, not a crypto wrapper.

The useful part.

An exchange publishes a score and a bet. Traders need to see both, at the same time, with the waiting orders behind the last price. The exchange’s own page is built to take the order. A separate screen is built to compare, replay and export. That split is real in shares, and it showed up again in prediction markets. It is the part of Leo’s Oddpool story that still holds.

The part that does not hold is “so the screen company stays independent and gets paid forever.” Oddpool’s next chapter is inside the exchange. Zora built the research view itself. ICE took the right to sell Polymarket’s odds. Tendex’s opening is the time before Forum, Noise or Polymarket cares enough to build this chart. That time is a window, not a moat.

Australian rules are a boundary, not a free pass.

Moneysmart, updated 3 August 2026, says no prediction-market operator is licensed as a financial market in Australia. It also points at the ban on binary options for ordinary investors. ASIC has said these bets are high-risk and that it is watching how local law applies. The communications regulator, ACMA, has already treated Polymarket as gambling and had it blocked. Calling Tendex a screen does not give anyone a licence to run an exchange. It also does not automatically make it legal to pay someone to send people into a blocked site. The thirty-day test should not recruit Australians into Polymarket. The United States is where the bets, the licences and the money are being argued.

This is a limit for the test, not legal advice. Someone qualified in the relevant place still has to read the actual referral before it is sent.
