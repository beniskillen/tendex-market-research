What prediction markets are allowed to teach

Four neighbours. One of them is the product.

Leo’s deck borrows status from prediction markets, from a search-and-stocks paper, from Bloomberg, and from meme coins. Each borrow is partly fair. The useful part is smaller than the sentence it arrived in.

AnalogueWhat transfersWhat does not
Prediction venues People will trade a contract on an event, at large notional, and institutions will pay to distribute the probabilities. ICE’s Polymarket investment is the clean exhibit. Susquehanna making markets on Kalshi is the liquidity exhibit. The volume. It is mostly sports, then politics and crypto. Cultural attention is inside “other,” and “other” is the remainder. A binary contract that resolves on a date is not a continuous index.
Prediction terminals The job “one schema, a tape, an API” has a posted price. Oddpool proved a two-person YC company can ship it and get a conversation with the venue. The conversation ended with them joining Kalshi. A durable independent subscription. New Oddpool plans are closed. YC marks the company inactive. The venue internalised the layer. That can happen to Tendex too.
Search volume and stocks Google search is a direct measure of retail attention, distinct from news and turnover. In Russell 3000 names, 2004 to 2008, a rise in search predicted higher prices over about two weeks and a reversal within the year. Strongest in small, retail-traded stocks. The Federal Reserve did not publish this result. The paper is Da, Engelberg and Gao, Journal of Finance, October 2011. The sample is seventeen years older than the 24-month freshness line; it stays because it is the paper people mean. It does not show that a cultural index hedges a brand, or that the effect survives after everyone can see it.
Research seats Koyfin’s own page shows a serious retail researcher paying $39 or $79 a month on an annual plan. Unusual Whales starts at $50 for a faster tape in options. There is a habit of paying for a view the broker does not give you. A $24,000 Bloomberg seat. Public pages do not agree on that price, and Tendex does not have the coverage, the compliance, or the installed base that rent pays for.
Meme coins Price can detach from cash flows and track what people are talking about. Distribution in crypto is a skill Ben has used elsewhere. The instrument. A token’s price is the attention. An index contract is supposed to settle on an outside series. Mixing them in a Forum conversation makes Tendex sound like a casino skin. Forum’s public posture is the opposite: a path toward a designated contract market, away from a crypto wrapper.

The mechanism worth keeping.

A venue publishes a series and a contract. Traders need to see both, in time, with the book behind the print. The venue’s own page is built to take the order. A terminal is built to compare, replay and export. That split is real in equities and it showed up again in prediction markets. It is the part of Leo’s Oddpool story that survives.

The part that does not survive is “therefore the terminal company stays independent and gets paid forever.” Oddpool’s next chapter is inside the exchange. Zora shipped the research view itself. ICE took the distribution right for Polymarket’s probabilities. The opening for Tendex is the period before Forum, Noise or Polymarket cares enough to build the overlay. That period is a window, not a moat.

Regulatory neighbour, not a hall pass.

Moneysmart, updated 3 August 2026, says no prediction-market operator is licensed as a financial market in Australia, and points at the binary-option ban for retail clients. ASIC has said bets on these markets are high-risk and that it is watching how local law applies. ACMA has already treated Polymarket as a gambling service and had it blocked. Calling Tendex a terminal does not license a venue, and it does not automatically make a paid introduction into a blocked venue lawful. The thirty-day test should not recruit Australians into Polymarket. The United States is where the contracts, the licences and the capital are being argued.

This is a boundary for the experiment, not legal advice. A person who is qualified in the relevant jurisdiction still has to read the actual referral before it is sent.

Paper: Journal of Finance, vol. 66, no. 5, October 2011.
