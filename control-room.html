The working file.

The decision, the sums, the meeting claims, and the decisions still not made. Cut-off 27 October 2026.

The question. Can Tendex attract the right trader with a useful attention-market screen, and can that bring in money through Forum? A second question: does any of that support a startup investors would fund? Forum being keen is not the same as investors being keen.

What is missing. Proof, not a lack of curiosity. The category is public. The buyer, the right to use the data, a live trade list, and Forum’s actual problem are not.

Ben’s own limit. A short unpaid trial with a cap. Hours, cash, who owns the work, and the stop date are not yet agreed with Leo. An earlier idea of five hours a week was not accepted as a rule.

What would change the recommendation

A live trade list

If Leo can show real orders, lined up in time with a named score, the product claim moves from a slogan to a tool. The buyer is still unproven.

A Forum problem, with a date

If a founder names the user and the action, the test in the 26 October note becomes the test. If they only want signups, treat it as a services pitch.

Someone pays

An invoice from Forum, or ten traders paying even $30, replaces the made-up 100 accounts. Until then, money Tendex has actually made in public is zero.
