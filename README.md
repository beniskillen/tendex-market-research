# Tendex market research

Internal research file. Cut-off 27 October 2026. Not for publication, and not a message to Leo or Forum.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The segment explorer is `matrix.html`. The evidence and calculation trail is `control-room.html`, with the same trail in `internal-control-room.md`. Machine-readable figures are in `data/research.json`.

Type and colour are taken from the live Tendex terminal: IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono, paper `#f5f6f8`, ink `#0e1420`, price blue `#2f54eb`, attention amber `#b5710c`. The matrix reads place, then problem, then the offer. Pools are different units and are not added.

Client figures from the 20 October meeting stay labelled as meeting claims. Public Tendex revenue is zero.
